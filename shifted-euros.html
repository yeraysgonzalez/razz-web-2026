project overview

Brand Identity for European Manual Performance Car Culture

our challenge

Create a brand identity that captures the spirit of European car tuning culture, emphasizing the raw driving experience of manual transmission vehicles while maintaining a clean, modern aesthetic. The identity needed to feel dynamic, recognizable, and authentic to automotive enthusiasts.

our concept

The concept behind Shifted Euros is rooted in the mechanical interaction between driver and car. Manual transmissions represent control, skill, and engagement—values that define this community. The visual direction draws inspiration from gear shifting mechanics, motion, and performance engineering, translating these ideas into a distinctive brand mark.

design strategy

The design focuses on communicating movement and performance through typography and symbolic elements.

The word-mark uses an italicized form to create a sense of forward motion and speed. This stylistic choice reinforces the dynamic nature of driving and automotive performance.

Complementing the word-mark, the icon is inspired by the gear shift pattern, one of the most recognizable symbols of manual driving. This graphic element strengthens the brand's connection to car culture and provides a versatile visual signature.

color palette

The color palette is bold and energetic, inspired by roads and motorsport aesthetics.

The icon is derived from the pattern of a manual gear shift

Transforming a functional mechanical reference into a memorable brand symbol.

brand in action

The Shifted Euros identity successfully translates the culture of manual European performance cars into a bold and recognizable visual system.

The italicized word-mark communicates motion and speed, while the gear shift–inspired icon creates an immediate connection with driving enthusiasts.

The final identity balances performance aesthetics and modern minimalism, allowing the brand to stand out across social media, automotive merchandise, and community-driven events.
